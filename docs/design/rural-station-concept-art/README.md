## Shop style study

The [station shop style study](station-shop-style-study.png) compares the same hall with an empty bench, plain collar and empty shelf on the left, and the cushion, bandana and model train fitted into their places on the right. The approved style uses broader color shapes, fewer surface details and crisper silhouettes. It keeps the hall layout, cast and sandwich gag. The game uses an empty base and separate prop layers, with the bench arm overlapping the cushion.

Built-in ImageGen used [the original station hall](station-hall.png) as the visual reference. The generation prompt was:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new landscape art-direction comparison for the original rural station game, using the attached station hall as the edit target for character identity, camera, architecture and palette. The old image is a layout and cast reference. Redraw it in a materially flatter 2D cartoon style. Show the new station scene twice, in two equal widescreen frames side by side on a wide landscape board, divided by one cream gutter. Same camera and artwork composition in both frames: teal wooden bench with Pitsi the charcoal-and-tan dog underneath in the front left, arched doorway and orchard, very large simple clock, open public floor, simple teal counter on the right with Dad and the two siblings. Simplify the busy background by roughly two thirds. Original graphic silhouettes, chunky playful shapes, hand-drawn irregular curves, dark teal outlines on characters and props, muted cream and teal environment, terracotta floor, one solid shadow tone at most per form, crisp edges. No woodgrain, weathering, tiny floor seams, rendered highlights, realistic light rays, gradients, heavy texture, 3D, or photorealism. Keep the dog's ears expressive and a tiny sandwich wrapper near his nose for one quiet wink. Left frame: plain bench, plain collar, short empty wall shelf. Right frame: selected cosmetics at those exact locations, a large plum oval cushion ON the bench with a two-tone seam, a small coral triangle bandana actually TIED around Pitsi's neck, and a toothy turquoise-and-ochre model locomotive actually resting on the wall shelf. The cosmetic objects share the scene's scale, flat fills, shadow logic, line weight and perspective, with strong silhouettes visible at small preview size. Keep all other positions and details identical as closely as possible. No floating badges, emojis, selection outlines, text, UI, labels, stylization samples, or watermarks. Original character designs only.

## Continuity for production art

Use the lineups as the character reference for the next iteration. The hall study varies some costume colors and uses a placeholder photograph and sign lettering. Make the photograph show the former sign above this same entrance when drawing the clue view. Give the Hebrew names and key evidence their own legible close-ups. Pitsi's charcoal and tan coloring, cream markings, pointed ears and coral neckerchief draw on the existing project illustration.

## Image provenance

All five PNGs were created for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool using text prompts. The art direction and the description of the existing Pitsi illustration informed the prompts. The generation calls used no input images. Each prompt below includes the same style paragraph at the point marked `[Shared style]`.

> Original game IP for ages 9–14 in a contemporary rural Hebrew-speaking village. Distinct, economical cartoon shapes and graphic poses with the warmth of a hand-painted 1990s point-and-click adventure. Sketch-to-polished 2D preproduction art, opaque color, warm dark linework, restrained texture. Use original designs only. Avoid franchise lookalikes, existing game characters, logos, signature head shapes, pixel art and photorealism.

### Family Lineup

Use case: stylized-concept. Landscape primary cast concept sheet on a warm cream paper field. [Shared style] One lineup with exactly three humans and one dog, full bodies with comfortably spaced overlapping-free clean silhouettes, drawn near their believable relative heights. From left: an early-teen brother with a soft angular face, dark curly hair, teal overshirt and ochre backpack, leaning forward with a notebook and an excited theory; an early-teen sister with a strong rounded-square face, deep brown hair tied high, brick red zip jacket and dark plum trousers, standing still as she notices a detail on a ticket, an amused side eye; their father, a kind station-counter worker with dark slightly graying hair, navy cardigan over a muted blue collar shirt with sleeves rolled, holding an ordinary cardboard parcel marked 27 with an understated raised eyebrow; and their family dog Pitsi, a small-medium standing sheepdog with a charcoal body and upright pointed ears, tan legs and rounded muzzle, cream blaze and chest, dark nose, coral neckerchief, held tail, one ear visibly twisting toward a tiny crumpled sandwich wrapper near his paws. Brother and sister plausibly related and of similar age. Design the funny moment so it reads in one glance. Flat colors in big clean blocks with one subtle shadow, very little ground shadow. Same floor line, generous surrounding margins. No titles, names, other writing, graphic panels or side thumbnails.

### Station Hall

Use case: stylized-concept. A wide landscape location painting, station hall at the exact moment of the parcel 27 case, composed as a playable fixed three-quarter point-and-click scene. [Shared style] Cream plaster walls, faded teal doors and trim, warm ochre late-afternoon light falling from the orchard entrance, broad clean values, textured painted background and flat-color sharp-edged characters. Believable rural village public station interior, warm and used, old mechanical clock, counter, a clear walkway, worn waiting bench with one dark chin-rest patch, and a single readable crooked tile at the open entrance. Parcel marked '27' on the counter is the visual focal point. Behind it Dad is a warm middle-aged man with a navy cardigan and muted blue collared shirt, eyebrow up. In front of it two early-teen siblings, brother with dark curly hair, teal overshirt and ochre backpack leaning into a theory, sister in brick red zip jacket with deep brown high-tied hair examining the label. Their small-medium charcoal-and-tan sheepdog Pitsi lies under the nearby bench, pointed ear turned toward a small sandwich wrapper, cream blaze and coral scarf. The café owner is entering at the far side holding a wide photo print. Keep figures comparatively small in the readable room, no HUD, no text other than parcel number, blank signboards are acceptable. The station hall needs space to walk around and a clear door silhouette.

### Station Cafe

Use case: stylized-concept. Wide painted location concept for a humble contemporary village station café that feels like a playable classic 2D point-and-click screen. [Shared style] Fixed three-quarter view from across the tiny room. Cream plaster, dusty sage and teal green joinery, honey wood and late ochre sunlight. Clear silhouettes for service counter, two or three mismatched tables, empty walkway to orchard-side glass entry, blue bench near the window, station clock glimpsed outside. One carefully framed sandwich award as an optional wink, shown by a simple sandwich emblem and gold seal on a tasteful cream certificate without lettering. A little framed photo next to it shows the original sandwich falling apart, with one tomato slice visibly escaping. Leave a worn spot for Pitsi at the bench as a secondary detail. Calm painted texture, prop placement that invites clicks, no people, restrained props, no readable text, no UI or logos.

### Village Locations

A cohesive landscape location board with four equal landscape thumbnail paintings in a clean 2 by 2 grid separated by narrow warm cream gutters, each a readable fixed-camera three-quarter point-and-click location from the same rural village. [Shared style] Consistent late afternoon lighting and cream plaster, worn teal wood, olive and orchard green, honey stone, terracotta accents, gold light. Top left: the village square, compact and slightly sloping, stone paving and a small anniversary bandstand with string lights, suitcase beside a drum as a visual wink. Top right: a footpath between orchard stone walls with a blue bench in one branch and a low unpainted stone gate in the other, distinct landmarks, village roofs in distance. Bottom left: the repair workshop, open teal door, one clean workbench with a lamp and tool silhouettes, warm oil-stained floor, a toy wheel temporarily propping up a radio for a visual wink. Bottom right: the former station building at the end of the public footpath, small light-plaster building with faded trim and a closed old public notice case, orchard and low hills beyond, a fence clearly separates it from any rail area. Grounded proportions and clear foreground/middle-ground/background in each panel. No people, labels, lettering, logos, UI or watermark.
