# Station menu and shop

**Design proposal · For review**

## The main menu · תחנת ראטן

Use the station hall and forecourt as the home screen. The siblings stand by the public noticeboard, Dad is at the counter, and Pitsi sleeps under the bench until the family looks ready to leave. An orchard wall and the path up to the square establish the village beyond the station. The scene can show one or two equipped decorations and react when the player previews a purchase. The menu heading is **תחנת ראטן** and the game header shows the German name **Kurort Rathen**.

Use a readable action panel on the right and the station illustration on the left. Keep navigation in labeled Hebrew buttons. The main story has one primary button. Offer a short, specific description of where a saved chapter will resume, such as **פרק 1 · בית הקפה**.

| Action | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **יוצאים לחקירה** | Start the parcel case when there is no chapter save to resume. |
| **ממשיכים בחקירה** | Resume the saved chapter at its last place and step. **פרק חדש** is a secondary action here, with confirmation if it replaces a chapter checkpoint. |
| **משחקונים** | Open the compact activity board. Show each available game with its name, place and activity. Put **ממשיכים** beside a game with a compatible saved run. Use the same named resume pattern for the detective case and rehearsal where their own saves exist. |
| **חנות התחנה** | Open the station shop to preview and equip items. Show the current bell balance on entry. |
| **צלילים** · **להורים** | Give audio and parent controls their own small row. Keep reporting, export and reset available. |

The activity board is a separate view. It can use two columns of short cards at 1024×600, with three cards per column for the six station proposals, or fewer if content is still in testing. For the first station build, the two previously hidden games appear after their designs pass a child playtest. Decisions about the Gatekeepers entry belong to its separate treatment. A clear **לתחנה** action returns to the menu, restores focus and keeps active runs available. At 1024×600 the main screen should fit with the save label, all controls and confirmation dialog. Test that view plus help and resume at 1280×720 and 1440×900. Moving decorations and character reactions respect pause and reduced motion.

Home's job is to help the child pick up the story or find a familiar activity quickly. On a paper sketch, give the child a chapter save and a spelling save. Ask them to return to the café in the chapter, resume the postman's activity, then change Pitsi's accessory. Compare the same menu with the saved place shown on the primary button or directly underneath it. Observe whether the child can tell which run will open.

## The station shop · חנות התחנה

The station has a small souvenir shelf beside the public counter. The player spends the existing **פעמונים** on family accessories and decorations. Preview puts the item on Pitsi or in its spot in the hall, entrance or square. The shop shows the cost, balance and status before purchase. Once owned, an item can be equipped, replaced or removed for free. Items are cosmetic and all story paths, help and learning tools are available without purchases.

Keep the current balance and earn rules initially so the first playtest can isolate whether the catalog gives the child a reason to come back. Preserve existing purchase records and progress when planning the switch. The carry-over decision is whether to bring compatible items such as Pitsi's hat or provide an exchange for farm-only items. Save compatibility needs its own decision before any catalog migration. A cosmetic should have its own placement and never cover a public notice, case clue or button.

*Animal Crossing: New Horizons* gives players visible expressions of their choices through clothing and decorations. Here, a small catalog with a live station preview and free equipment changes gives the child an early choice and a larger thing to save toward. [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/animal-crossing-new-horizons-us-109505/)

Ask what the child would buy first, where they expect it to appear, and what they would save for next. Compare the same item with a live preview and a still thumbnail at the same price. When testing price, compare two prices for one item with the same balance and preview.

## Pitsi in the family

Pitsi is the family dog and stays recognizable as the charcoal-and-tan dog from the current game. Reuse his pose and reactions where they fit. He gives the siblings reasons to stop and look, waits beside the station bench while Dad works, leads the way on public paths, and is generally wrong about whether the sandwich is for him. The siblings follow up on what he notices. His main menu spot, the two trail games and the cameo roles in rehearsal and Noga's case make him part of everyday station life. The postman's role stays distinct from Dad's counter work. See the [station mini-game proposals](rural-station-mini-games.md).
